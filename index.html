Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about joining and participating in your neighborhood network.

Is nebenan.de free to use?
Yes! nebenan.de is 100% free for neighbors. You can create an account, browse your local feed, join community groups, and chat with neighbors at zero cost.
Who can see my posts and profile?
Only verified neighbors within your local area or fellow members of the specific community groups you join can see your posts. You can also customize your profile privacy settings at any time in the app.
Can I create my own local community group?
Absolutely! Anyone can create a community group for their neighborhood (such as gardening, running, childcare, or emergency assistance). You can choose to make it public to all neighbors or invite-only.
How do I reach support if I need help?
Our support team is always ready to assist. You can reach out directly via email at support.nebenan.de@gmail.com.
